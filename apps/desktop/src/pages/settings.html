About

Lumen runs on Matrix, the open standard for secure, decentralised messaging. Conversations are end-to-end encrypted by default, you verify people and devices by comparing emoji, and your key backup opens only with your recovery key. Because it's Matrix, there's no lock-in: you can talk with people on other Matrix servers and use other Matrix apps such as Element.

Lumen servers include WireGuard for private administration of the server. Your chats don't travel through it: they're protected end to end by Matrix.
