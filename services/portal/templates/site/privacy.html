Privacy policy

This policy describes what {{ brand.legal_name }} ("we") processes when you use {{ brand.name }}, written to match what the software actually does.

1. The short version

  • Messages and files in encrypted rooms (the default) are encrypted on your devices. We cannot read them.
  • We process account data (username, email, sign-in records) and messaging metadata (who is in which room, when) to run the service.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, or use analytics trackers. No third-party scripts run on our pages.

3. Federation

When you join a room that includes people on other Matrix servers, those servers receive the room's events — encrypted message content plus the metadata listed above — and store them under their own policies. That is how federation works; we cannot delete data from other servers.

4. Processors

If online payments are enabled, Stripe processes payment details as our processor; card numbers never reach our servers. If email is enabled, our email provider processes the messages we send you. We use no other third parties to process your data.

5. Your rights

You can view and export your account data and delete your account from the Privacy & data page. Account deletion deactivates your Matrix account, asks other servers to forget your profile, and removes your account data after the cancellation window. For other requests contact {{ brand.privacy_email }}.

See our security page for how data is protected, and its limits.

7. Changes

We'll announce material changes in the app and on this page before they take effect.
