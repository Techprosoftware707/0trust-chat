Security

Vulnerability disclosure policy

We welcome reports from security researchers and will work with you to fix issues quickly.

How to report

Email {{ brand.security_email }} with a description, the affected component and steps to reproduce. You can also use the contact form with the topic "Security report". Our security.txt lists current contacts.

Scope

  • This website, the customer portal, the web chat app and the homeserver at {{ lumen.server_name }}.
  • The Lumen installer and operational scripts.
  • Issues in upstream components (Synapse, the Matrix SDKs, vodozemac, Caddy) are welcome too; we'll coordinate with the upstream project.

Please don't

  • Access, modify or delete other people's data, or degrade the service (no denial-of-service tests).
  • Use social engineering, physical attacks or spam.
  • Test against other Matrix servers you don't own.

What we commit to

  • Acknowledge your report within 3 business days and keep you updated.
  • Not pursue legal action for good-faith research that follows this policy.
  • Credit you in our release notes, if you'd like.
