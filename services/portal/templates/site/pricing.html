Billing questions

What is a workspace?

A team space for billing and administration: members, roles, invitations and policies such as mandatory MFA. Members get the workspace's file limits in every chat.

Who processes payments?

When online payments are enabled, Stripe handles card details; they never touch our servers. We receive the subscription status through signed webhooks.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then the workspace moves to the Free plan's limits.
