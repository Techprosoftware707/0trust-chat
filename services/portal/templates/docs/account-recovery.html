Your account and your encryption keys are recovered separately.

Forgot your password

{% if lumen.email_enabled %}Use password reset; we email you a link.{% else %}This server doesn't send email, so password reset by email isn't available. Contact support for assisted recovery.{% endif %}

Lost your MFA device

Sign in with one of your recovery codes instead of a code from the app, then set up MFA again. Without recovery codes, contact support: after verifying your identity, an operator can issue a one-time recovery link. Every such action is recorded in the audit log and shown in your security history.

Lost your recovery key

If you still have a signed-in, verified device, create a new recovery key from Settings → Security in the chat app. If you don't, encrypted history from before can't be recovered by anyone. You can reset your encryption identity to keep using chat; contacts will see an identity-change warning and should re-verify you.

Assisted recovery can't restore messages

Restoring access to your account does not restore your message keys. That's what keeps them private.
