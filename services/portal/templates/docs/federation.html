{{ brand.short_name }} is built on Matrix, the open standard for secure, decentralised messaging. That is why you can talk to people on other Matrix servers and use other Matrix apps. How Matrix works.

Messaging someone on another server

Start a new chat with their full address, for example @sam:example.org. Their server must be reachable and allowed by this server's federation policy ({{ lumen.federation_mode }}).

Encryption across servers

End-to-end encryption works the same across servers. Verify people on other servers exactly as you would locally.

Using another Matrix app

In a Matrix app, choose a custom homeserver and enter {{ lumen.server_name }}. The app discovers the homeserver automatically and signs you in through your {{ brand.short_name }} account.

Limitations

  • Servers in a room keep their own copy of its history and metadata.
  • If the other person's server is down, messages are delivered when it comes back.
  • Room names and topics are not end-to-end encrypted.
